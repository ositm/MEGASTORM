# LabLink — Elevator Pitch

## The one-liner

**LabLink is the logistics and trust layer for diagnostic testing in Nigeria — patients book and pay for lab tests online, samples travel through a GPS-tracked, tamper-evident chain of custody, and results arrive digitally from verified laboratories.**

## The 30-second pitch

Getting a lab test in Nigeria is broken. Patients don't know which labs to trust, pay cash at a counter, queue for sample collection, and wait days — sometimes forever — for a paper result. Labs, meanwhile, have no digital channel to reach patients or manage their fulfilment.

LabLink fixes the whole journey. A patient finds a verified lab near them, books online, and pays securely with Paystack. They can walk in — or a vetted phlebotomist comes to their home, tracked live on a map like a ride-hailing driver. Every handoff of their sample, from collection to courier to lab bench, is recorded in a cryptographically linked chain of custody that nobody can quietly edit. The lab processes the sample in its own portal, a supervisor validates and releases the result, and the patient is notified in-app and by email the moment it's ready.

We're live today: a deployed patient platform, a deployed laboratory portal, real payments, real emails, and self-service onboarding for every participant — patients, collectors, couriers, and labs — with admin verification gates protecting the network's trust.

## The 10-second version

"Book a lab test like you'd book a ride — verified labs, doorstep sample collection, tamper-evident tracking, digital results."

## Why now, why us

- **Everything is already built and deployed** — this is not a deck, it's a working product with a full test suite behind it.
- Diagnostics demand in Nigeria is growing while trust in results remains the #1 barrier — chain of custody is our moat.
- The marketplace flywheel: every verified lab adds patient supply; every patient adds lab demand; collectors and couriers earn per job.
